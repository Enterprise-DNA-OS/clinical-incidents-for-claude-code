# attention

Show what needs a person today: every legal, policy and house clock that is overdue or due in seven days (HDC Right 10, the Victorian duty of candour, HQSC Part A and B, open disclosure, ratings, reviews, actions, risk reviews), then late reports, incidents with no manager, open sentinel events and extreme risks.

Run `node scripts/incidents.mjs attention`. Add `--json` when you need every field. Resolve the service, staff member or record by name first; if the CLI says a name is ambiguous, list the candidates and ask. Replace every angle-bracket value with a fact from the records or the operator. Never run a placeholder.

Lead with the law, then national policy, then house rules. Give one next step per line (usually `/acknowledge`, `/disclose`, `/notify`, `/rate`, `/start-review` or `/draft-acknowledgement`).
