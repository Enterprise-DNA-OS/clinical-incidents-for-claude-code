# Why this target

Picked on 27 October 2026 as a new family for the Enterprise DNA rebuild series: clinical incidents and feedback for health providers.

## The incumbent and the bill

RiskMan describes itself as "the incident and risk management system of choice across Australia and New Zealand" for over 20 years. It is now part of RLDatix, which says it remains committed to "the thousands of users who rely on RiskMan every day" and lists "Migration Options": move to RiskMan Cloud, or explore DatixCloudIQ, its "next-generation platform" ([rldatix.com/en-apac](https://www.rldatix.com/en-apac/solution/risk-safety/riskman/), checked 27 October 2026).

There is no public price. RLDatix is listed as aimed at mid-market and enterprise organisations, with quote-based pricing ([erpresearch.com](https://erpresearch.com/erp-add-ons/healthcare/rldatix)). Every service pays a private quote each year, priced on its size and modules.

## Why it fits

- **Data-shaped.** Incidents, people affected, ratings, disclosure steps, reviews, actions, feedback, risks, outside notifications.
- **The rituals are weekly:** rate the new reports, chase the clocks, prepare the committee pack, follow up complaints, review the risk register.
- **The rules are specific and dated:** HDC Right 10 working days, the Victorian duty of candour, HQSC Part A and B, the Australian sentinel events list. Every one is a clock, and clocks are what a database is good at.
- **A switching moment.** RiskMan customers are being offered a migration either way.

## What stays with a custom build

The two-minute staff reporting form on the ward, anonymous reporting, a phone app, and the investigation forms each service has built in RiskMan.
